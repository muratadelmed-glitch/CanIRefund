FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about return policies and the checker.

How accurate is CanIRefund?

We verify all return policies directly from official retailer sources and update them regularly. Each policy shows the last verification date so you know the information is current.

Is this service really free?

Yes! CanIRefund is completely free to use. We believe shoppers deserve clear, accurate information about return policies without any cost.

What if my item is past the return deadline?

Some retailers offer exceptions for defective items or have extended return windows for loyalty members. Always check the official policy or contact customer service directly.

Do I need a receipt to return an item?

Most retailers require proof of purchase, but policies vary. Our checker shows receipt requirements for each store and category.

Can I return opened items?

It depends on the retailer and product category. Some stores accept opened items within the return window, while others require items to be sealed. Our checker accounts for item condition.

Who pays for return shipping?

Return shipping policies vary by retailer. Most major stores offer free return shipping for eligible items, but some may charge restocking fees or require you to pay for shipping.
